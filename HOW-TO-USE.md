# How to use AI Sight

**Booklet source for print, Braille, and illustration.**  
This file is the full user guide. Give it to another AI or designer to turn into a printed booklet, large-print pages, or Braille. Keep the steps and command phrases exactly as written.

**App name:** AI Sight  
**Who it is for:** Blind and visually impaired people, plus family or caregivers who help them.  
**Tagline:** See more. Live better.

## 1. What AI Sight does

AI Sight is a camera app that talks.

It can:

1. **See objects** in front of the camera and say what they are (object detection).
2. **Describe** the scene in a few spoken sentences.
3. **Read** a page, sign, menu, or screen out loud.
4. **Call** family with voice or video, inside the app.
5. **Message** family (text, photo, voice note, map).
6. **Guide walking** to a place, if walking directions are turned on.
7. **Call for help** in an emergency.

The person using it mostly **holds the screen and speaks**. Family can also tap buttons.

There are two kinds of account:

| Account | Who | After sign-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blind person** | The person the app is helping | Opens the live camera |
| **Assistant** | Family, friend, or caregiver | Opens Contacts, to chat and call |

## 2. Put the app on the phone (first time)

**Do this (iPhone):**

1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Open the AI Sight website your helper gave you.
3. Sign in (or create the account first — chapter 3).
4. Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Name it **AI Sight**.
6. Open it from the home-screen icon after that.

**Do this (Android):**

1. Open Chrome.
2. Open the AI Sight website.
3. Use the browser menu **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
4. Open it from the home-screen icon.

**Need internet** for describe, read, calls, messages, and maps. Object detection can keep working on the phone after it is downloaded once.

**Illustration:** phone home screen with one AI Sight icon. Caption: “Open this icon every time.”

---

## 3. Create an account

### Blind person

1. On the welcome screen, choose **Blind person**.
2. Tap **Create an account** if you do not have one.
3. Enter first name, surname, username, email, phone, and a password (at least 8 characters).
4. Optional: health notes (for example diabetes or low vision).
5. Add **emergency people** (name, phone, relationship). You can add more than one.
6. Tap **Create account**.

The app gives you a **System ID** and a **QR code** (also in Settings). Family needs this to link as an assistant.

### Assistant (family)

1. On welcome, choose **Assistant**.
2. Create an account, or sign in if the blind person already created you as a contact.
3. If you register yourself, type the blind person’s **System ID** (example: `AIS-XXXXXX`), or **Scan QR**, or pick a QR photo.
4. Choose who you are (son, daughter, mother, father, family, friend, caregiver, other).
5. After sign-in you land on **Contacts**, not the camera.

The blind person can also create an assistant from **Contacts** by tapping **+** and filling in their name, username, email, phone, password, and relationship.

**Illustration:** two phones side by side. Left: camera. Right: contacts list. Caption: “Blind person uses the camera. Family uses Contacts.”

---

## 4. Sign in and stay signed in

1. Choose Blind person or Assistant.
2. Enter **username** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

You stay signed in until you tap **Sign out** in Settings.

If you already signed in, opening the home-screen icon goes straight to the camera (blind person) or Contacts (assistant).

## 5. First-time allow: camera, microphone, location

The first time the camera opens, the app asks:

**Allow camera, microphone, and location.**

**Do this:**

1. Tap that button.
2. When the phone asks, tap **Allow** (camera, then microphone, then location).

After that, they start by themselves whenever you open the app, until you sign out.

**iPhone home-screen app:** if location never turns on, go to iPhone **Settings → AI Sight → Location → While Using the App**. Also turn on **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services**.

**GPS pill** on the camera: yellow/on when location is working.

**Illustration:** one large button on the camera: “Allow camera, microphone, and location.”

## 6. The camera home (main screen)

This is the screen the blind person uses every day.

**Whole screen is a button.** You do not need to find a small mic icon.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold** the screen, then speak, then **let go** | Listens, then does the command |
| **Double tap** | Start or stop **object detection** |
| Short tap | Starts a shorter listen (if hold is not used) |

When it is listening you hear **Listening...**

The app may say: *Hold the screen to speak. Double tap for object detection.*

### Top of the camera

- **Logo** (left): AI Sight. Admin can replace this picture.
- **Profile** (right): opens Profile.
- **GPS**: location on or off.
- **DET**: object detection. It should not spin “LOAD” every time after detection is already on the phone.

### Bottom dock (left to right)

1. **Camera** (circle) — this home screen  
2. **Navigation** (arrow) — walking / destination  
3. **Contacts** (two people) — people, chat, call  
4. **Profile** (person) — your details  
5. **Plans** (star) — Free vs Premium  
6. **Settings** (gear) — calling, updates, voice  

**Illustration:** full-screen camera. Caption callouts: “Hold to talk” in the middle, “Double tap = detect” , dock labeled 1–6.

## 7. Voice: the one rule

**Hold the glass or the phone screen. Speak clearly. Let go.**

Use the person’s **name as saved in Contacts** (for example “my brother”, “Mama”, or “Ravi”).

If it did not hear you, hold again and say it shorter.

You can also say **help** or **what can you do**.

The app answers with:

*Hold the screen to speak. Double tap for object detection. Say send a message, send a photo, or send a map. Say what is in front of me, describe what is in front of me, or read this. Say open calling settings to set up voice and video calling once.*

**Say this:** `help`

## 8. Object detection (Free)

Object detection names things in the live camera: person, chair, phone, bottle, and similar everyday objects. It speaks them out loud and can draw boxes.

**First time on this phone (helper):**

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **On-phone object detection**.
3. Tap **Download YOLO to this phone**.
4. Wait until it says it is ready. Do this on Wi‑Fi.

After that, detection runs on the phone. Do not download again every day.

**Every day:**

1. Point the camera forward.
2. **Double tap** the screen.
3. The app says object detection started and begins speaking what it sees.
4. **Double tap** again to stop.

**Say this:** `detect`  
**Say this:** `detect objects`  
**Say this:** `is there a chair`  
**Say this:** `stop` (stops detection if it is running)

Do not cover the camera. Hold the phone steady.

**Illustration:** double-tap arrows on the camera. DET pill glowing.

## 9. Describe the scene (Premium when subscriptions are on)

Describe tells you the place and the main things in view, in a few sentences. Point the camera where you want it to look.

**Say this:** `what is in front of me`  
**Say this:** `describe what is in front of me`  
**Say this:** `what do you see`  
**Say this:** `look around`  
**Say this:** `I can't see`

The app should say *Okay. Looking in front of you.* then speak the description on the **loud speaker or Bluetooth**, not the tiny earpiece.

If it says describe is on Premium, open **Plans** (chapter 16). If the organisation has turned subscriptions off, describe stays free.

**Illustration:** person facing a kitchen. Caption: “Hold. Say: what is in front of me. Let go.”

## 10. Read a page or sign (Premium when subscriptions are on)

Hold the page, menu, sign, or phone screen **filling most of the camera**. Keep it still.

**Say this:** `read this`  
**Say this:** `read this for me`  
**Say this:** `what does this say`  
**Say this:** `read the page`  
**Say this:** `read the menu`  
**Say this:** `read the sign`

The app should say *Okay. Reading the page in front of the camera.* then read the words out loud.

If there is no text, it will say it cannot see a page to read.

**Illustration:** paper held in front of the camera, filling the frame.

## 11. Bluetooth and the speaker

Turn the phone **ring switch on** (iPhone) and turn **volume up**.

- **Object detection** and **walking directions** speak on the loud speaker and work well with Bluetooth.
- **Describe** and **Read** should also speak on the loud speaker / Bluetooth. Wait for the “Okay…” line, then the result.

**Settings → Test speaker** (helper):

- Test all  
- Test voice  
- Test detection  
- Test describe  
- Test reading  
- Test directions  
- Test calling  

If you hear the test, that part of the speaker is working.

**Illustration:** phone + Bluetooth earbuds. Caption: “Volume up. Ring switch on.”

## 12. Calling (voice and video)

Calls stay **inside AI Sight**. You do not log in to another website.

### One-time setup (helper)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Calling**.
3. Tap **Configure calling**.
4. Stay signed in to AI Sight. Do this once.

You can also hold the camera and:

**Say this:** `open calling settings`  
**Say this:** `set up calling`  
**Say this:** `configure calling`

**Test calling** and **Test video calling** are in Settings.

### Every day

**Say this:** `call my brother`  
**Say this:** `voice call Mama`  
**Say this:** `video call Ravi`

Use the name as it is in Contacts.

On **Contacts**, each person has buttons to **voice call** or **video call**. Tap the person to open chat.

### During a call

On the call screen:

- **Green tick** — answer  
- **X** — decline  
- **Mic** — mute  
- **Camera** — turn video on or off  
- **Red hang up** — end the call on **both** sides  

**Say this:** `stop` (ends the call if one is happening)

Incoming calls can reach you while the app is open.

**Illustration:** call bar with hang-up in red. Caption: “Hang up ends both sides.”

## 13. Contacts, chat, photos, maps

Open **Contacts** from the dock (two people).

- Tap **+** to add an assistant (blind-person account).
- Tap a person to open **Chat**.
- Chat tools: picture, voice note, location pin, typed message, send.

From the **camera**, you can speak instead:

**Say this:** `send a message to my brother`  
Then say the message when it asks.

**Say this:** `send a photo to Mama`  
**Say this:** `send a map`  
**Say this:** `send my location to my brother`  
**Say this:** `send a voice note to Ravi`  
**Say this:** `read my messages`  
**Say this:** `any new messages`

If you do not say a name, some send commands go to **everyone** in contacts. Prefer saying the name.

**Say this:** `delete contact Ravi`  
(It will ask you to confirm.)

## 14. Walking directions and the map

Walking directions are **off** until a helper turns them on:

**Settings → Walking directions (experimental) → Save settings.**

If they are **off** and you ask to go somewhere, the app offers to **send your location to family** instead. That is safer for many users.

If they are **on**:

**Say this:** `take me to Jagat Market`  
**Say this:** `navigate to the temple`  
**Say this:** `go to the bus stand`  
**Say this:** `where am I`  
**Say this:** `how far`  
**Say this:** `next turn`  
**Say this:** `show the map`  
**Say this:** `stop` (stops navigation)

You can also tap the **Navigation** dock button, type a place, and tap **Start navigation**. There is **Stop** and **Repeat** on that page.

The map can sit on the camera. Close it with the **×** or:

**Say this:** `close the map` (or the app’s close-map command)

To hear the map again: **Say this:** `show the map`

**Illustration:** street map with a blue dot (you) and a red pin (destination).

## 15. Emergency

**Say this:** `emergency`  
**Say this:** `SOS`

(The app treats emergency / SOS as a help command.)

What happens:

1. It says it is getting your location.
2. It can share location with emergency people if that setting is on (default on).
3. It tries to **call** your primary emergency contact inside the app.
4. If the in-app call cannot start, it can fall back to the phone **tel:** dialer.

**Settings:** **Share location during emergency** — leave on unless a helper has a reason to turn it off.

Emergency people are added at registration and shown on **Profile**.

This does not replace local emergency numbers (police, ambulance). Family should still save those on the phone.

**Illustration:** simple SOS word, not graphic images.

## 16. Plans (Free and Premium)

Open the **star** on the dock.

**Free** (typical):

- Object detection  
- Video and voice call  
- Messaging  

**Premium** (typical, about **$5 / month**):

- Describe  
- Read  

If the organisation turns subscriptions **off** in Admin, everything stays free.

If you say describe or read on a locked plan, the app says to open Plans and subscribe.

Pay with the processor the organisation chose (**Stripe** or **PayPal**) when those keys are set.

**Illustration:** two cards. Free / Premium. Star icon.

- Speech language

## 19. Assistant day-to-day

1. Sign in as **Assistant**.
2. You see **Contacts** and the linked person.
3. Open chat, send texts, photos, voice notes, maps.
4. Voice-call or video-call from the contact buttons.
5. Open **Profile** to see health notes and System ID.
6. You do not use the live detection camera the same way; the **blind person’s phone** is the camera.

Keep the app open or return to it so you can receive calls and messages.

## 21. Troubleshooting (helper)

| Problem | Try |
| --- | --- |
| App looks old | Settings → **New update**. Wait for the site to update, then reopen. |
| Camera / mic / GPS ask every time | Allow once. Stay signed in. Do not sign out. iPhone: Settings → AI Sight → Location. |
| Detection always loading | Settings → download YOLO on Wi‑Fi. Double tap only after it is on the phone. |
| Describe / read too slow | Point the camera, hold still, wait for “Okay…”. Need internet. Try again. Check Premium if asked. |
| Describe / read in the earpiece | Volume up, ring switch on, Bluetooth connected. New update. Test describe in Settings. |
| Call does not start | Settings → Configure calling (once). Both people signed in. Internet on. |
| Hang up did not end their side | Use the red hang-up in the app, not only the phone’s side button. |
| Map / GPS missing | Allow location. Safari home screen. Walking directions switch. |
| Cannot hear anything | Test speaker in Settings. Unmute the phone. |
| Signed out by accident | Sign in again. Allow camera, mic, location once more if asked. |
| Assistant cannot link | Use the exact System ID or QR from the blind person’s Settings. |

## 22. Safety notes for the booklet

- AI Sight helps; it can be wrong. Cross-check streets and medicines with a person when you can.
- Object detection names common objects; it is not a medical or legal reader.
- Describe and read need a clear photo and internet.
- Emergency contact is family you chose, not a government emergency service unless you dial them yourself.
- Keep the phone charged. Use a neck strap or glasses mount so the camera points forward.
